# Casa Aurelia — Client Agreement

Original Phase 23 deliverable (Steps "Agreement" and "Deposit" of the
First-Client process in `docs/Client_Delivery_Process.md`). A short, plain-language
agreement that commits the agreed **scope, price, timeline, and payment terms**
once the client says yes — distinct from `docs/Proposal_Template.md` (which
proposes) and `docs/Client_Delivery_Process.md` (which sequences the work).

Fill in the fields marked with `[ ]` per client. This is a template, not legal
advice; adjust to your jurisdiction/business.

> **Honest rule (no fabrication):** fill only real agreed values. If no client
> exists yet, this template stays blank — do not invent clients or deposits.

## 5. Responsibilities

**Provider agrees to:**
- Build the website to the agreed scope, on the agreed timeline.
- Provide one review round with corrections (see proposal).
- Keep the Client informed of progress and any issues.

**Client agrees to:**
- Provide the agreed content on time.
- Pay the deposit and balance as agreed.
- Review the site and give timely feedback.
- Not make scope changes without a written change request.

---

## 6. Change requests

Any change to the agreed scope requires a written change request noting the change
and any impact on price and timeline, approved by both parties before work begins
on it.

---

## 7. Hosting & maintenance

Ongoing hosting and care (if agreed) covers hosting, backups, security updates,
menu/content updates where agreed, bug fixes, and support, per
`docs/Sales_Materials.md`. Where not agreed, the Client manages updates via the
admin dashboard and is responsible for their own hosting.
